REFERENCE DETAILS

Transparent by design.

What this prototype does

Matches your supplied expectations and payments by an exact, case-sensitive reference. Amounts are calculated in integer cents. Multiple payments are added together; negative payments reduce the total.

What the numbers mean

Difference = expected amount minus net linked payments. Open difference sums positive balances only. Overpayments and unlinked payments are shown separately; they do not offset open differences.

Zero expectations are treated as reversed. A zero expectation with a nonzero net payment needs reversal review. Payment dates after the report date are rejected. Aging starts at the expected row’s date, not a contractual due date.

What it does not establish

No MFP eligibility calculation, manufacturer payment verification, payer connection, contract interpretation, or claim recovery. A matched amount confirms arithmetic, not correctness of the expectation or receipt of bank funds.

Privacy and session storage

Files are processed in this tab’s memory. This workspace has no analytics, file uploads, external fonts, or browser storage. Reloading clears imported records. Downloaded reports remain on your device. Standard site hosting can still record page requests.

This prototype is for synthetic or appropriately de-identified data. It has not been assessed for production handling of patient information.
