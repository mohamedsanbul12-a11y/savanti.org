The Problem

Every day, someone leaves without the medication they came for.

You learn to recognize the pause. The prescription goes in, the price comes up on the screen, and the patient goes quiet for a second. Then they say they will come back for it later.

The discounts that would have fixed it were sitting right there the whole time. They are just spread across two dozen different websites, each with its own eligibility fine print, and nobody can work through that in the ninety seconds a patient is standing at the window.

Savanti puts all of it on one screen, for any drug, in seconds.

The cards are everywhere and nobody can find them
GoodRx, ScriptCycle, SingleCare, TrumpRx, and dozens of manufacturer programs all offer real savings. But patients have to find them one by one, on their own, under pressure.
Pharmacists can't always catch every option
A pharmacist filling 400 prescriptions a day can't manually search every coupon source for every patient. They want to help. The workflow does not give them room to.
Skipping medication has real consequences
When patients can't afford blood pressure, diabetes, or cholesterol medications, they do not just skip a pill. They turn up in the emergency department a few months later, with something much more expensive to treat.

How It Works

How it actually works.

You do not need an account, and you do not need insurance.

Step one
Search your medication
Type in your drug name and dosage. Savanti instantly pulls up every available discount card, coupon, and manufacturer program.
Step two
Compare all your options
See GoodRx, ScriptCycle, SingleCare, TrumpRx, and manufacturer assistance programs side by side. The best price is flagged automatically.
Step three
Show it at the counter
Show the card or coupon to your pharmacist and they run it at the register. Nothing to sign up for.

Why I Built This

Five years behind the counter.

Before pharmacy school I spent five years in retail pharmacy. I lost count of the number of people who said they would come back for a prescription and never did. Some of them told me straight out that it was the medication or the groceries that week.

The discounts were always there. Nobody could find them fast enough. GoodRx, manufacturer copay cards, patient assistance programs: all real, all on separate sites, none of them reachable in the moment that actually matters.

That is the whole reason Savanti exists. One place to check every option for your prescription, before you ever get to the counter.
