Our Story

Built from
the counter up.

Before pharmacy school, Mohamed spent five years working the retail pharmacy counter — watching patients walk away from prescriptions they couldn't afford, fielding calls from people lost in a system that wasn't built to help them.

Those years made one thing clear: the gap between clinical intent and patient outcome isn't a knowledge problem — it's a tools problem.

Now in pharmacy school, Mohamed is combining that ground-level experience with clinical training in pharmacokinetics, therapeutics, and HEDIS metrics — alongside a growing foundation in healthcare business strategy. Savanti is where all of it converges.
