The Story Behind Savanti

Mohamed Mohamed

PharmD Candidate 2027
Atlanta, Georgia

Founder

Built from the counter up

Before pharmacy school, Mohamed spent five years working the retail pharmacy counter — fielding calls from confused patients, watching people walk away from prescriptions they couldn't afford, and seeing firsthand how the system fails people at the exact moment they need it most.

Those years made one thing clear: the gap between clinical intent and patient outcome isn't a knowledge problem — it's a tools problem. Pharmacists care deeply. They just don't have the right systems to act on that care at scale.

Now in pharmacy school, Mohamed is combining that ground-level experience with rigorous clinical training — pharmacokinetics, therapeutics, HEDIS metrics, and guideline-directed care — alongside a growing foundation in healthcare business strategy. Savanti is where all of that converges: a platform designed by someone who has lived both sides of the equation.
