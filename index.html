The Problem

Patients are walking away from medications they need — every single day.

Nobody sees this more clearly than the pharmacist at the counter. A patient hands over their prescription, the price comes up, and they go quiet. Then they say they'll come back later. They don't.

The discounts exist. The assistance programs are out there. But they're scattered across dozens of websites, buried in fine print, and impossible to find in the 90 seconds someone is standing at a pharmacy window.

Savanti puts every discount card, coupon, and manufacturer program in one place — visible in seconds, for any drug.

Discount cards are everywhere — and invisible
GoodRx, ScriptCycle, SingleCare, TrumpRx, and dozens of manufacturer programs all offer real savings. But patients have to find them one by one, on their own, under pressure.
Pharmacists can't always catch every option
A pharmacist filling 400 prescriptions a day can't manually search every coupon source for every patient. They want to help — the workflow just doesn't allow it.
Skipping medication has real consequences
When patients can't afford blood pressure, diabetes, or cholesterol medications, they don't just skip a pill — they end up in the ER. This is preventable.

Why I Built This

I watched it happen at the counter, every day.

Before pharmacy school, I spent five years working the retail pharmacy counter. I watched patients go quiet when the price came up. I watched them say "I'll come back" — knowing they wouldn't. I watched people choose between their medication and their groceries.

The discounts were always there. The problem was nobody could find them fast enough. GoodRx, manufacturer programs, PAP assistance — they exist. But scattered, buried, and impossible to navigate in real time.

Savanti is built to change that. A single place where any patient can see every option for their prescription — before they ever reach the counter.
